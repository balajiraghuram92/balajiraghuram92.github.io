Week 5 homework: AI Evals in Practice

H1

Homework 1 · AI Evals in Practice · Chapter 1

Why AI evals

  • An eval is a repeatable test that shows if a feature is good enough for real users
  • Leaderboard scores don't replace testing your own product
  • Scope first: problem, baseline, metric and a clear pass or fail rule

LabRun ch1_01_first_eval.py, then ch1_04 to catch the judge changing its answer when the order is swapped
